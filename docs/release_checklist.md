# Before sharing the study publicly

- This public repository is subject to updates as the associated publications progress.
- Confirm artwork/backing photograph, map-layer attribution and consent for any figures showing precise sampling positions or individual identifiers.
- Confirm authorship, acknowledgments, final citation, deposit and separate licenses. Cite the manuscript and data accessions as they become available.
- Reconcile the description of fold coordinates with the saved analysis. Keep exploratory demographic fits and provisional dates separate from the main results.
- Recover the complete reads-to-ref5 procedure and any missing filtering, TreeMix and dependency records before claiming independent reconstruction.
