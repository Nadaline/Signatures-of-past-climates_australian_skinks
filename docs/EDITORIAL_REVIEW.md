# public repository and editorial handoff

I retain seven archived main figures, the R6 baseline-demography supplement and the other available non-pilot chapter supplements, with selected rendering and analysis sources and aggregate results. Figure 7 is candidate A, not the Stage 11 comparison or candidate B.

Candidate A was regenerated from its archived model and SFS values. Its PNG is pixel-identical to the selected original; no demographic fit was rerun. The seven original PDFs matched the Drive catalogue by filename and size. The catalogue did not expose a comparable checksum, so local versions are pinned separately by SHA-256. Other figure renderers were checked for source correspondence and R syntax, not executed end to end.

I do not include raw data, heavy objects, exact locality tables, specimen metadata, manuscript drafts or run logs. Archived figure proofs can show sampling information and are for private review only until public redistribution is cleared. The older geographic sample summary was withheld because its range fields and group–state mapping need review. The complete R7 text was not independently verified.

I use the original illustration in the private README. The draft pocket guides and badges are no longer part of the repository presentation. Public rights are documented separately in the [reuse status](../LICENSE-STATUS.md).

The recovered copy was rebuilt from the permanent local Chapter 2 and R6 archives. The external drive was not mounted during that recovery, so optional copies of the original TreeMix shell and the moments fitting modules were left out. Their archived figure sources and aggregate results are included; the paths and dependencies for a complete analytical rerun still need review.

The [private review repository](https://github.com/Nadaline/Lampropholis-delicata-genomic-history-staging) is separate from the original project. It is not approved for public release.
